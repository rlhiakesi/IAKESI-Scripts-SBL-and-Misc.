IAKESI Manga Production Script Format

General

The production script should contain only information needed to produce
the manga pages.

Do not include design notes, production commentary, brainstorming notes,
or explanations to the reader.

Dialogue Rules

Every spoken dialogue block is enclosed in a single pair of parentheses.
All dialogue belonging to one speaker remains inside that same pair of
parentheses.

Single Speaker

(Speaker: “Dialogue”)

Example:

(Seraphine: “Welcome to the Royal Academy of Magic.”)

Connected Dialogue Bubbles (same speaker)

(Speaker: “Dialogue 1” -> “Dialogue 2”)

Shared Dialogue Bubble

(Speaker 1, Speaker 2: “Shared dialogue”)

Multiple Speakers in the Same Panel

Use parentheses to indicate comic-book reading order.

Example:

(Seraphine: “Lady Midori.”) (Midori: “Yes, Lady Seraphine?”)

Internal Thoughts

(Character (thinking): “Thought”)

Example:

(Midori (thinking): “This place is much larger than the Temple.”)

Narration

Narration: “Text appearing in a narration box.”

Example:

Narration: “Two mons later…”

Setting

Setting:

Describe the artwork for the panel or scene.

These instructions are for the image generator only and do not appear in
the finished artwork.

Do not place Setting text inside quotation marks.

Sound Effects

Sound: Rinnnnng

Sound: Tap

Sound: Fwoosh

Render environmental sound effects in canonical Zoran geometric script
where appropriate.

Visual Direction

Describe what is visible.

Do not write production commentary such as:

-   “This tells the reader…”
-   “This establishes…”
-   “Add comedy here…”
-   “Opportunity for humor…”

Instead, simply script the scene.

Camera Direction

Include camera direction naturally within the Setting description.

Script Organization

Chapter

Act

Page

Scene

Example:

Chapter 11

Act 1 — Arrival at the Royal Academy

Page 1

Scene 1

Setting: …

Narration: “…”

(Seraphine: “…”)

(Midori: “…”)

General Production Rules

-   Separate image-generation instructions (Setting) from narration.
-   Narration always appears in narration boxes.
-   Dialogue always follows the dialogue syntax above.
-   Thoughts use the (thinking) syntax.
-   Do not include production commentary or brainstorming notes.
-   Write only what is needed to generate the artwork.
