IAKESI — CHAPTER REVISION COMMANDS (VERSION 5) COPY/PASTE CHECKLIST FOR
CHATGPT =====================================

HOW TO USE Replace [CHAPTER] with the chapter number. Attach the current
working DOCX, the current Assets ZIP, and the global production
specification as needed. Paste ONE numbered command at a time into
ChatGPT. Work from the newest verified DOCX, not an older revision.

DEFAULT RULE FOR EVERY COMMAND: Use the latest verified working script
as the sole editorial master. Preserve approved dialogue, narration,
visual beats, and stable source IDs unless I explicitly authorize
changes. Make the requested changes to the DOCX, then rerun all affected
checks and repair errors introduced by your changes. Return the updated
DOCX and a concise PASS/FAIL/REVIEW report. Do not open previewers or
render artwork unless I explicitly request it. Never claim a full pass
when only structural checks have been performed. Do not proceed to the
next step with unresolved blockers. At SBL generation and release,
validate all mandatory schema fields at the panel level, not only at
page/chapter level; a successful render prompt is not schema evidence.

============================================================ PHASE A —
STORY AND EDITORIAL INTEGRITY
============================================================

STEP 01 — SOURCE INVENTORY AND BASELINE SNAPSHOT

COMMAND TO PASTE: Perform Step 1 for Chapter [CHAPTER]: Source inventory
and baseline snapshot. Identify the authoritative chapter DOCX, current
canon/global production setup, assets archive, and reference versions.
Count pages/scenes/dialogue units and record checksums; flag missing
references and obsolete alternatives. Establish stable
scene/utterance/visual-beat IDs before structural editing.

STEP 02 — WHOLE-STORY AND CONTINUITY PASS

COMMAND TO PASTE: Perform Step 2 for Chapter [CHAPTER]: Whole-story and
continuity pass. Check chronology, motivation, causality,
chapter-to-chapter continuity, and established story canon.

STEP 03 — CHARACTER-ARC PASS

COMMAND TO PASTE: Perform Step 3 for Chapter [CHAPTER]: Character-arc
pass. Track important characters independently, including trust,
motivation, competence, and relationship changes.

STEP 04 — MIDORI PSYCHOLOGICAL-DEVELOPMENT PASS

COMMAND TO PASTE: Perform Step 4 for Chapter [CHAPTER]: Midori
psychological-development pass. Verify that her conclusions about
promises, dependence, authority, competence, trust, and power are earned
by shown events. Adapt this character-specific pass when a chapter’s
central arc differs.

STEP 05 — HISTORICAL AND CULTURAL PASS

COMMAND TO PASTE: Perform Step 5 for Chapter [CHAPTER]: Historical and
cultural pass. Check period-specific institutions, literacy, clothing,
food, etiquette, hierarchy, and language. Record deliberate manga
conventions separately from accidental anachronisms.

STEP 06 — WORLD AND CANON PASS

COMMAND TO PASTE: Perform Step 6 for Chapter [CHAPTER]: World and canon
pass. Check DOTG, Soul Relocation Area, Terra/Zorna responsibilities,
reincarnation, mana, Zora/Nyssara and chapter-dependent rules. Resolve
contradictions before layout.

STEP 07 — REDUNDANCY AND OBSOLETE-MATERIAL CLEANUP

COMMAND TO PASTE: Perform Step 7 for Chapter [CHAPTER]: Redundancy and
obsolete-material cleanup. Remove superseded alternatives, duplicated
instructions, abandoned notes, and contradictory production guidance.
Keep only approved canon.

STEP 08 — DIALOGUE/NARRATION EDITORIAL PASS AND COMIC SCRIPT SYNTAX
NORMALIZATION

COMMAND TO PASTE: Perform Step 8 for Chapter [CHAPTER]:
Dialogue/narration editorial pass and COMIC SCRIPT SYNTAX normalization.
Check voice, period appropriateness, exposition, repeated lines, and
artwork-versus-dialogue responsibilities. Enforce named speaker,
connected-chain ownership, thought syntax, and all-caps lettering policy
without changing words unintentionally.

STEP 09 — EDITORIAL INTEGRITY GATE

COMMAND TO PASTE: Perform Step 9 for Chapter [CHAPTER]: Editorial
integrity gate. Compare scene, utterance, and visual-beat IDs against
baseline. Confirm no unauthorized omissions, duplicates, or dialogue
edits. Freeze approved story content, not pagination.

GATE: Do not advance unless all required checks pass or I explicitly
approve documented exceptions.

============================================================ PHASE B —
PAGE DESIGN AND LETTERING CAPACITY
============================================================

STEP 10 — PRODUCTION SPECIFICATION INTAKE

COMMAND TO PASTE: Perform Step 10 for Chapter [CHAPTER]: Production
specification intake. Load current IAKESI Global Production Setup and
chapter-specific rules. Establish canvas, trim, safe areas, page parity,
artwork/lettering/text separation, CSP authority, styles, prohibited
text, and guide-specific exceptions.

STEP 11 — QUANTITATIVE TEXT-DENSITY BASELINE

COMMAND TO PASTE: Perform Step 11 for Chapter [CHAPTER]: Quantitative
text-density baseline. Measure dialogue, thoughts, narration, captions,
and display labels by scene and proposed page; count words, characters,
bubble chains, speakers, and anticipated balloon footprints. Flag
outliers before layout.

STEP 12 — PROVISIONAL PAGE/PACING AND MINI-ARC PASS

COMMAND TO PASTE: Perform Step 12 for Chapter [CHAPTER]: Provisional
page/pacing and mini-arc pass. Place story beats into provisional pages.
Assess page turns, silent beats, mini-arc openings in panel 1, and
whether scenes earn their space. Do not freeze pagination.

STEP 13 — PROVISIONAL PANEL LAYOUT AND PANEL-COUNT RHYTHM

COMMAND TO PASTE: Perform Step 13 for Chapter [CHAPTER]: Provisional
panel layout and panel-count rhythm. Plan panel-to-scene/visual-beat
mapping; evaluate panel-count histogram and repeated-grid runs. Preserve
major moments, reaction beats, and chronological ordering.

STEP 14 — MANDATORY PANEL-GEOMETRY DIVERSIFICATION PASS

COMMAND TO PASTE: Perform Step 14 for Chapter [CHAPTER]: Panel-geometry
and visual-rhythm diversification, BEFORE Step 15. Using the complete
provisional panel/scene/visual-beat map, assign explicit top-to-bottom
row patterns for EVERY page (e.g., 1-2-1, 2x2, 2-1-2, 1-2-2, or a
justified full-width arrangement). Evaluate both panel-count runs AND
geometric-layout runs; these are distinct checks. Generate a complete
page-by-page layout-pattern inventory, distribution/histogram, longest
repeated-pattern runs, and a numbered contact sheet covering the actual chapter page count. Identify excessive horizontal-strip pages,
repeated grids, and missed opportunities for action, reaction, establishing
shots, and dramatic emphasis. Introduce varied full-width, two-column,
and asymmetric layouts where the STORY and lettering allow; do not force
a pattern or arbitrary quota, split a connected bubble chain, create empty
panels, or sacrifice reading order, visual clarity, or artwork space merely
for variety. Preserve approved story beats, dialogue exactly once, panel
IDs and page count unless a documented fit/story reason requires reflow.
For each page, record the chosen pattern and story-specific justification,
including any intentionally repeated or all-full-width layout. Output
provisional numeric rectangles with correct recto/verso safe areas and
nonoverlapping gutters, plus a page-to-panel geometry map. Do NOT mark
this step PASS from panel counts alone or from a schematic that omits
lettering burden. Carry its approved geometry directly into Step 15.

GATE: Do not advance to Step 15 without a complete page-by-page
geometric-rhythm audit and explicit review of intentional exceptions.

STEP 15 — ARTWORK-AWARE GEOMETRY AND LETTERING-FIT PASS

COMMAND TO PASTE: Perform Step 15 for Chapter [CHAPTER]: Artwork-aware
geometry and lettering-fit pass. Start from the Step 14 diversified
numeric rectangles; do not silently replace them with uniform horizontal
strips. Estimate balloon fit at permitted font sizes for EVERY panel,
including narrow columns, connected speech chains, narration, and SFX;
check speaker visibility, tail routes, reading order, gutters, and art
negative space. Revise geometry or add pages rather than silently
shrinking lettering. Preserve scene/utterance/beat IDs.

STEP 16 — CONTROLLED EXPANSION/REFLOW AND PAGE-TURN REGRESSION

COMMAND TO PASTE: Perform Step 16 for Chapter [CHAPTER]: Controlled
expansion/reflow and page-turn regression. If pages are inserted,
transform the complete editable DOCX, not only SBL geometry. Recalculate
parity, safe areas, page turns, scene ownership, supplement pagination,
and dialogue exactly-once mapping. Re-run Step 14 geometric-layout
rhythm and Step 15 density/fit checks on every affected page. Prefer
even-number additions where useful, never at the expense of
storytelling.

STEP 17 — LAYOUT APPROVAL GATE

COMMAND TO PASTE: Perform Step 17 for Chapter [CHAPTER]: Layout approval
gate. Require explicit per-page panel mapping, complete action and
dialogue ownership, no unexplained orphan/silent panels, no
overlaps/out-of-bounds rectangles, acceptable estimated lettering fit,
and reviewed page turns. Verify BOTH panel-count and geometric-layout
rhythm against Step 14, with justified exceptions; reject a layout
that reverts to repetitive strips without review. Lock pagination only
after this gate.

GATE: Do not advance unless all required checks pass or I explicitly
approve documented exceptions.

============================================================ PHASE C —
DIRECTING, CONTINUITY, AND ASSETS
============================================================

STEP 18 — DIRECTORIAL PASS

COMMAND TO PASTE: Perform Step 18 for Chapter [CHAPTER]: Directorial
pass. Establish Setting, Action, Direction, Camera, Blocking,
Expression, Lighting, Effects and relevant Props for each page/panel,
using documented inheritance where appropriate. Distinguish narration
boxes from production-only directions.

STEP 19 — VISUAL-CONTINUITY PASS

COMMAND TO PASTE: Perform Step 19 for Chapter [CHAPTER]:
Visual-continuity pass. Track characters, costumes, persistent props,
crystal/gauge states, lighting, geography, season, time of day,
eyelines, entrances/exits, and movement between panels and pages.

STEP 20 — ASSET INVENTORY, CREATION, AND CLASSIFICATION

COMMAND TO PASTE: Perform Step 20 for Chapter [CHAPTER]: Asset
inventory, creation, and classification. Compare recurring
characters/costumes/locations/important props to the current archive.
Create missing canonical sheets only where necessary; classify
canonical, supplemental, and generated-on-the-fly material. Include
language/script reference documents in the asset package when required.

STEP 21 — TWO-LEVEL ASSET-BINDING PASS

COMMAND TO PASTE: Perform Step 21 for Chapter [CHAPTER]: Two-level
asset-binding pass. Bind scene context and panel-visible references
separately using exact Assets/ paths. Check presence, suitability,
authority, non-visibility, duplicates, and excessive references. No
generic replacement for a known canonical asset.

STEP 22 — FULL PAGE/PANEL PRODUCTION PREFLIGHT

COMMAND TO PASTE: Perform Step 22 for Chapter [CHAPTER]: Full page/panel
production preflight. For every page and every panel verify complete
source content, Setting/Action/Direction/Camera/Blocking (explicit or
unambiguous inherited), cast visibility, props, assets, lettering
ownership, geometry, and safe area. Check unique source IDs and exact
content preservation. Produce page-level and panel-level reports with
blockers distinguished from warnings.

STEP 23 — SCRIPT FREEZE GATE

COMMAND TO PASTE: Perform Step 23 for Chapter [CHAPTER]: Script freeze
gate. Require zero unresolved blockers, user-approved exceptions, a full
DOCX integrity report, and a frozen filename/hash. No SBL generation
before this gate.

GATE: Do not advance unless all required checks pass or I explicitly
approve documented exceptions.

============================================================ PHASE D —
SBL AND RENDERING
============================================================

STEP 24 — SBL CONVERSION FROM FROZEN SCRIPT

COMMAND TO PASTE: Perform Step 24 for Chapter [CHAPTER]: SBL conversion
from frozen script. Generate the SBL as a deterministic derivative.
Preserve stable source IDs and exact page/panel assignments. Never use
the SBL as an independent editorial master. REQUIRED PER-PANEL RENDER
STYLE: Populate both `image_style` (nonempty string) and `render_style`
(object containing nonempty `description`) on EVERY panel in the base
SBL and final SBL, including silent panels, flashbacks, and supplements.
Use the current global/chapter production style, including any approved
panel-specific exception; do not invent a style override. For a chapter
whose approved default is full-color realistic manga, each panel must
contain `"image_style": "full-color realistic manga"` and
`"render_style": {"description": "full-color realistic manga"}`.
The page-level style, global metadata, or external render prompt may
supplement but NEVER replace these panel-level fields. Carry the same
values into the per-page render JSON/package. Verify panel counts and
source/geometry/asset preservation after conversion. Compare EVERY
converted panel rectangle with the Step 14/14/16 approved geometry;
fail if the SBL converter silently regenerates uniform-strip layouts.

STEP 25 — SBL RELEASE VALIDATION

COMMAND TO PASTE: Perform Step 25 for Chapter [CHAPTER]: SBL release
validation. Verify schema, required field types, numeric geometry,
page/parity/safe areas, asset paths and archive contents, character
visibility, dialogue exactly-once ownership, all visual beats, and no
silent omissions. MANDATORY STYLE SCHEMA GATE: Iterate EVERY panel of
both the base SBL and final SBL and require a nonempty string
`image_style` plus an object `render_style` with a nonempty string
`description`. Check that the two values agree with each other and with
the approved global/chapter style or explicitly documented panel
exception. Validate the SAME fields in each generated page render JSON
and render package. Fail closed on missing, null, empty, mistyped,
contradictory, or merely page-level declarations; report exact page and
panel IDs. Never mark a style-schema warning as PASS. Verify three
coordinated per-page files: art PNG, transparent lettering-overlay PNG,
and exact text TXT. Verify CSP lettering workflow, registration,
full-color rendering, print resize/crop and folio separation. Compare
SBL against frozen DOCX. Compare the base SBL, final SBL, and per-page
render JSON geometry with the approved Step 14/14/16 page-by-page
layout map; report missing/mismatched panel rectangles and layout-pattern
regressions by page/panel ID.

STEP 26 — RELEASE GATE BEFORE RENDERING

COMMAND TO PASTE: Perform Step 26 for Chapter [CHAPTER]: Release gate
before rendering. Require machine-readable PASS for all structural
checks, including the per-panel image-style schema and a representative
per-page render-package round-trip test, plus documented human review
of semantic/layout risks. No rendering on warnings disguised as passes.
If a render attempt reports a missing required field, repair the SBL
conversion/validation process for the entire chapter, regenerate all
affected JSON files and page packages, and rerun Steps 24–25 before
rendering resumes.

GATE: Do not advance unless all required checks pass or I explicitly
approve documented exceptions.

STEP 27 — RENDER-REVIEW AND PRODUCTION QA

COMMAND TO PASTE: Perform Step 27 for Chapter [CHAPTER]: Render-review
and production QA. Render only after release. Compare output to script
for character appearance, action, dialogue tails, lettering, camera,
blocking, continuity, art style, and safe area. Fix structural problems
in the DOCX and regenerate dependent SBL; do not repeatedly prompt
around structural defects. Validate art/lettering registration and print
pipeline before final delivery.

============================================================ MANDATORY
CHANGE-CONTROL COMMAND (USE AFTER ANY REVISION)
============================================================

Compare the new Chapter [CHAPTER] DOCX with the last approved version.
Verify all stable scene, dialogue, narration, and visual-beat IDs;
compare exact reader-visible text and intended action. Report additions,
deletions, duplicates, and moved material. Recheck every dependency
affected by the change, including pagination/parity, geometry, geometric-layout rhythm, Step
14 pattern inventory, lettering, asset bindings, continuity, and
SBL correspondence as
applicable. Repair any new errors and repeat validation until no
blockers remain. Return the verified DOCX.

============================================================ WHEN YOU
NEED TO ADD PAGES
============================================================

Perform a controlled page expansion of Chapter [CHAPTER] in the complete
working DOCX. Preserve every approved dialogue unit and visual beat
exactly once. Reassign scenes and panels, create explicit
silent-continuation instructions where needed, recalculate all
downstream page numbers, recto/verso parity, safe areas, page turns, and
guide placement, and recheck lettering fit AND Step 14 geometric-
layout diversity. Prefer paired page
insertions when useful, but prioritize storytelling. Do not modify
geometry JSON alone. Verify the resulting DOCX contains the actual scene
content on the correct pages, not merely headings.

============================================================ FINAL
REMINDER ============================================================

A panel-count-rhythm PASS does not establish geometric-layout diversity.
The Step 14 layout inventory and Step 15 lettering-fit validation must
precede layout approval; SBL geometry must preserve the approved map.
A structural PASS is not a complete production PASS. Freeze only after
content preservation, panel direction, asset suitability, lettering fit,
page parity, and production requirements have been verified.
