COMIC SCRIPT SYNTAX

Speech:
(Character: "text")
One speech bubble. Tail points only to that character.

Multiple speech bubbles:
(Character: "text 1"
-> "text 2"
-> "text 3")
A chain of connected speech bubbles belonging to ONE speaker.
There must be ONLY ONE tail on the connected chain, and it must point to that character.

Thought:
(Character (thinking): "text")
Cloud-shaped thought bubble.
Thought dots point only to that character.

Multiple thought bubbles:
(Character (thinking): "text 1"
-> "text 2")
A chain of connected thought bubbles.
Only one chain of thought dots points to that character.

Never attribute dialogue to any character not named.
Never draw an extra bubble tail.
Never draw a thought bubble unless '(thinking)' is specified.

Font:
Comic Lettering Standard (STRICT):
Use a clean, rounded comic sans-serif lettering style matching Chapter 11 Page 4.
Use ALL CAPS.
Do not use bold text.
Do not use italic text except for emphasis.
Maintain consistent letter size throughout the page.
All speech bubbles on the page must use the same lettering style. Do not vary fonts, weight, capitalization, or style between bubbles.

Panel Composition
Never crop off the speaking character.
Always show the speaker unless the script explicitly requests an off-panel voice.
Keep speech bubbles inside the panel boundary.
Do not overlap faces with speech bubbles unless unavoidable.
Preserve left-to-right reading order for speech.


CANONICAL REFERENCES (always included)

The attached character sheets are the sole authoritative references for Adnil, Naandeen, and Zarleen.

These attributes are locked:
• face construction
• hair color/style/length
• eye color
• body build
• skin tone
• academy uniform
• proportions
• posture and demeanor

If the comic page differs from the reference sheets, correct the page to match the reference sheets.

Do not make any other creative changes.

Background Extras (Canon)
Background students are anonymous and must never resemble the principal cast.
Female Zorans may vary in hairstyle (bob, shoulder-length, waist-length, braided, 
ponytail, bun, etc.) but always retain platinum-blonde hair in varying shades from pale 
platinum to warm champagne blonde.
Male Zorans always have white hair with varied hairstyles.
Heights, builds, facial structures, and skin tones should vary naturally.
Background characters should never become the visual focus unless called out by the script.
Population realism: Unless a character is specifically identified as a major recurring 
character, background people should be generated with realistic variation in attractiveness, 
age, physique, facial symmetry, and grooming. Anonymous crowds should resemble a cross-section 
of ordinary people rather than a cast of idealized models.
Avoid "fashion catalog syndrome."
The students should resemble a real population rather than a cast of models.
Approximately:
15% exceptionally attractive
35% above average
30% average
15% plain
5% distinctly unattractive
Include natural diversity in:
face shape
nose size
jaw shape
body proportions
posture
hairstyle
age appearance
height
weight
Some students should be:
slightly overweight
stocky
lanky
awkward
freckled
asymmetrical
tired-looking
acne-prone
older-looking
less fashionable
Avoid making every anonymous student young, slim, symmetrical, and conventionally beautiful.
